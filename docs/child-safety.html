Child Safety Standards

校友汇 AlumniHub · Standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)
儿童安全标准 · 禁止儿童性虐待与性剥削（CSAE）

1. Our commitment / 我们的承诺

校友汇 AlumniHub (“AlumniHub”, “we”, “us”) has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We prohibit any creation, sharing, solicitation, storage, or distribution of CSAE/CSAM on or through the app.

校友汇 AlumniHub 对儿童性虐待与性剥削（CSAE）及儿童性虐待材料（CSAM）采取零容忍政策， 严禁在应用内创建、传播、索取、存储或分发相关内容。

2. Eligibility / 使用资格

AlumniHub is an alumni networking service for adults. It is not intended for children under 18. Users must be at least 18 years old to create an account or use the service. We remove accounts we determine belong to underage users.

本应用面向成年校友社交，不面向未满 18 岁的未成年人。我们发现未成年人账户时将予以移除。

3. Prohibited conduct / 禁止行为

  • Any sexual content involving minors (including fictional or AI-generated depictions)
  • Grooming, solicitation, or attempts to engage minors in sexual activity
  • Sharing, requesting, or linking to CSAM
  • Using AlumniHub to facilitate CSAE offline or on other platforms

禁止任何涉及未成年人的性内容（含虚构或 AI 生成）、诱导/招揽未成年人、分享或索取 CSAM， 以及利用本应用协助线下或其他平台的 CSAE。

4. How to report / 如何举报

If you encounter suspected CSAE, CSAM, or other child-safety concerns in AlumniHub, report them immediately:

  • Email our child safety contact: elwinxcheng@gmail.com (subject line: Child Safety / CSAE Report – AlumniHub)
  • Include the relevant profile, conversation, content link or screenshots, and the approximate time of the incident when possible

如发现可疑 CSAE/CSAM 或其他儿童安全问题，请立即发送邮件至 elwinxcheng@gmail.com，并尽量提供相关账号、对话、内容与时间信息。

5. What we do after a report / 处理流程

When we obtain actual knowledge of CSAE/CSAM or a credible report, we will:

  • Review the report promptly
  • Remove violative content and restrict or permanently ban responsible accounts
  • Preserve evidence as required for lawful investigations
  • Report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline and/or the appropriate regional authority, as required by applicable law
  • Cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement

在获知或收到可信举报后，我们将及时审核、删除违规内容、限制或封禁账户，依法留存证据， 并向 NCMEC CyberTipline 和/或相关地区主管机关报告确认的 CSAM，并配合合法执法请求。

6. Legal compliance / 法律合规

AlumniHub aims to comply with applicable child safety laws and regulations in the jurisdictions where we operate, including obligations related to CSAM reporting and removal. These standards are published to meet Google Play’s Child Safety Standards policy for social apps and to inform our users of our CSAE prohibitions and processes.

此联系人负责接收与 AlumniHub 平台 CSAE 内容相关的通知，并协调审核与处置。
